How this visualization works

Every tablet is a dot placed by two of: find-spot longitude and latitude, approximate date (period midpoint), genre, and translation state. Slide between maps and the dots travel; anything you select stays selected.

The moving dots on the home page are a sample. Each dot is one tablet, but not every tablet: 116,813 are shown, a random 60,000 drawn from all 462,298, plus every tablet whose English (human or machine) contains barley, silver or sheep (65,250; some are in both groups), so those example searches show complete results. Every word match has English, so tablets with English make up far more of those dots than of the corpus. The percentages in the home-page legend are shares of the dots shown; for the whole corpus see Stats. This page, by contrast, draws every tablet.
