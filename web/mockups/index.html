Unread Atlas · map interface mockups

Exploratory prototypes, not part of the live site. They use the live search API and static JSON built from corpus.sqlite (scripts/mockups/).

A · Full-screen map + floating searchJust the map and a search box; filters slide out of a drawer, results summarised in a compact chip. B · Linked timeline histogramThe time slider as a second map: per-century bars for the query; brush a window to filter the dots, pan the map to update the bars. C · Gap viewEach find-spot as a ring split into human / machine-only / ATF-only / catalogue-only tablets, ranked by untranslated share. D · Word trail & research leadsA plant word (tarmuš, aktam) traced across sites and periods, with how translators render it and a ranked list of unread tablets that use it. E · Map of mapsEvery tablet is one dot with several coordinates (place, date, genre, translation state). Drag around the ring to slide between maps; dots travel continuously, the search and your selection come with you. F · Landing pageThe front door: the map-of-maps tours itself (geography → time × geography → time × translation state) behind the headline and search; any click hands over control. Below: preview cards, a featured research lead, a random tablet, and a collaborate block. G · Sign dictionaryOne page per cuneiform sign: search by name, reading, ATF or pasted Unicode; forms across periods in the eBL fonts plus the archaic drawing, readings by period from the corpus, English words that travel with it, top words, find-spots, and carefully labelled related scripts.
