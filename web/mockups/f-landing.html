What plant was tarmuš?

Translators render {ú}tar-muš as lupin in 32 published translations. It turns up on 214 tablets, mostly Neo-Assyrian medical recipes, but the medical tablets from Assur have never been read in English. These three share the most plant names with the translated texts:
