About this reader

A browser for new English readings of cuneiform works that have ATF transliterations but little or no open English on CDLI — literary, royal, scientific, omen, and letter corpora first.

  • Explore — filter ranked candidates from the local CDLI dump
  • Epic hunt — large literary / phrase-scored shortlist (Gilgamesh II, hymns, …)
  • Remedies — plant recipes and therapeutic tablets (head, teeth, eyes, belly…)
  • Omens — physiognomy, birth, skin spots, liver extispicy, Mul.Apin / astral
  • Math — reciprocal (igi), metrology, multiplication (a-rá) school tables
  • Map & time — findspots and a period scrubber
  • Reader — English · ATF · period-style cuneiform side by side

Direct links: Gilgamesh II · Head fever remedies · Mole physiognomy · Liver extispicy · Reciprocal table · Metrology

Cuneiform is drawn in a period-matched face, following the eBL converter (Neo-Assyrian, Neo-Babylonian, Old Babylonian cursive / literary / monumental, Hittite; Noto otherwise); the reader's “Cuneiform face” menu overrides it for your session (font credits). Offline ATF→Unicode uses a vendored eBL reading map; optional live conversion hits the eBL signs API.

Translation model integration (local T5 from atf-translate-test, or in-browser Hugging Face later) is the next layer — the English column is ready for line-aligned output.
