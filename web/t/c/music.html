Music

Texts about making music in Mesopotamia: laments sung by the kalû-priests to drum and harp, a catalogue of the lamenters' repertoire, and an Old Babylonian liturgy with its song sections. The English is an unedited machine draft; instrument names (balaĝ = harp or drum; ub, lilis) are rendered inconsistently. The famous tuning and notation tablets have no transliteration in our corpus yet; they are listed below as coming soon.
