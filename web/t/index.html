Research

Front door to curated texts and the growing Sumeripedia topic pages. Hand-picked tablets with machine translation, grouped by theme. Each text page is plain HTML: transliteration (where licensing allows), line-by-line machine English, and metadata.

  • Remedies

    9 tablets

    Medical prescriptions from Assyrian and Babylonian healers: plants, oils, beer and the occasional incantation, with instructions for preparing and applying them.

  • Omens

    8 tablets

    “If… then…” observations from Mesopotamian divination: birds, rivers, the moon and sun, dreams, births, and the entrails of sacrificed sheep.

  • Epic Hunt

    9 tablets

    Narrative and mythological fragments: gods in council, monsters defeated, floods and a goddess's self-praise.

  • Math

    8 tablets

    School and working mathematics from the 3rd to 1st millennium BCE: multiplication and reciprocal tables, squares, field and canal computations, and a gold-alloy problem.

  • Love & Fertility

    6 tablets

    Rituals and recipes for restoring a man's desire (ŠÀ.ZI.GA, “rising of the heart”), and Babylonian love lyrics, several from the cult of Marduk and his consort Zarpanītu.

  • Music

    6 tablets +5 coming soon

    Texts about making music in Mesopotamia: laments sung by the kalû-priests to drum and harp, a catalogue of the lamenters' repertoire, and an Old Babylonian liturgy with its song sections.
