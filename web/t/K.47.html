1If in Nisannu (Month I) a flood goes and the river like a flood....
2If the river is like a sick person's...: there will be illness in the river.
3If a river is like a death-snake: rejoicing.
4If a river is filled with famine: after the year Adad will devastate.
5If the river is like the water of a mountain: in the Apsu Ea an eclipse will take place.
