Catalogue of laments and prayers (K 2529)

machine translation machine English only

Nineveh tablet listing the incipits of balaĝ and eršemma laments with a total for each god. The machine English garbles the totals (e.g. “total: 39th year of Enlil” for a count of 39 compositions).
